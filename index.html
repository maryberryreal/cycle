Get to know your cycle

Four phases, four seasons

A typical 28-day pattern. Your cycle and experience may be different.

Winter Menstruation Days 1–5 +

Hormones Estrogen and progesterone are at their lowest.

In your body

  • The uterine lining sheds
  • Cramps or back pain may happen
  • Fatigue or weakness may occur

How you might feel

  • A greater need for rest and quiet
  • Lower energy
  • A sense of emotional release
Spring Follicular phase Days 6–13 +

Hormones Estrogen gradually rises.

In your body

  • An egg matures in the ovary
  • The uterine lining starts to rebuild
  • Your skin may feel clearer or firmer

How you might feel

  • Motivation and mental clarity may rise
  • More social or creative energy
  • A stronger sense of optimism
Summer Ovulation Around day 14 +

Hormones Estrogen peaks and luteinizing hormone (LH) rises.

In your body

  • An egg is released from the ovary
  • Cervical mucus may become thinner
  • Some people feel one-sided mid-cycle pain

How you might feel

  • More confident or outgoing
  • Energy or libido may increase
  • You may feel more attractive
Autumn Luteal phase Days 15–28 +

Hormones Progesterone is dominant, then falls toward the end.

In your body

  • Body temperature may rise slightly
  • PMS symptoms, breast tenderness, or fluid retention may occur
  • Cravings or bloating may happen

How you might feel

  • Physical and mental energy may decrease
  • Irritability or mood changes may occur
  • You may want to slow down

These are common experiences, not rules. Cycle timing varies, and phase estimates should not be used as birth control.
